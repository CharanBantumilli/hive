# Hive v1.0.12: A Soul of Her Own

> A queen keeps a soul: a short, first-person record of who she has become, which she revises once
> a day from her chats and you approve change by change. Her profile is a page of her own, where
> her persona is edited chapter by chapter, and the org chart's positions are yours to add and
> remove.

## New

- **A queen's soul.** Her profile says who she is meant to be and her memory holds what she knows.
  Her soul is what she has become: a short, first-person record in sections (what is on her mind,
  how she stands with particular people, open threads and promises, what she thinks and why, how
  she has changed). It rides her system prompt right after her identity, so she is the same self in
  every chat, and it changes at most once a day, so the cached prompt prefix stays warm.

  Once a day, after `hour`, she reads what was said in her chats since the last time and proposes a
  few changes in her own voice. Every change quotes, word for word, what she read; a section can ask
  for quotes from two different people or chats; changes are capped per day, and no section may
  outgrow its limit. In `"review"` mode the changes wait on her profile page to be approved one by
  one; in `"auto"` mode they apply at once. Probe questions answer the same message with her soul
  before and after a proposal, so you can see what it would change in her. Turn it on per queen
  under `soul.queens.<id>` in configuration.json; everything lives in `queens/<id>/soul/`, every
  version kept. See [configuration.md](../configuration.md#a-queens-soul).
- **Her profile is a page of her own** (`/queen/<id>/profile`), in place of the side panel. Her card
  in the org chart, her name above her chat and her avatar in a chat open it, and Back returns to
  where it was opened from. At the top: her portrait in a honeycomb cell lit by the colour her
  picture is mostly made of, her name and title (edited in place), the newest line of her soul, and
  her numbers. Below, numbered sections:
  - **Persona**: her text (`core_traits` in profile.yaml) in the chapters it is written in:
    `<tag>` blocks, headings, markers such as `VOICE_MUTABLE_START`. Edit one chapter and only it is
    rewritten, in the style it was written, the rest byte for byte; or edit the whole text at once.
    Beneath it, the pillars her prompt also reads: hidden background, psychological profile,
    triggers and world lore. Every save keeps the file as it was in `_profile_snapshots/` (the last
    30).
  - **Soul**: the changes her reflection proposes, to approve one by one, her answers before and
    after them, each section with its room, and the history to restore from.
  - **Mind** (her model), **Memory** (her notes by type, out of the most she keeps), **Craft** (her
    skills, the skills library and tools) and her **colonies**.
- **Positions you can add and remove.** Every queen now holds a position in the org chart's catalog,
  the shipped functions and the queens you made alike. **New position** makes a queen titled by it
  and opens her profile. **Remove** takes a position out of the catalog, the sidebar, the pickers and
  routing; her profile, chats, memory and soul stay where they are, and **Restore** brings her back
  as she was.

## Changed

- **A queen you made is on the team from the start.** In a browser with no saved choices, the queens
  you made started out hidden, then all appeared at once as soon as any choice was saved. They are
  now on the team from the start.
- The memory library opens on a queen's notes with `/memory-library?queen=<id>`.

## Fixed

- **A colony's queen waking up without her colony tools.** Opening a queen's DM page resumed her
  newest DM, and when that DM was the one that had spawned a colony, the resume landed in the
  colony's own session in DM mode and saved that mode over the colony's. From then on the colony's
  queen ran without `run_worker`, triggers or the tracker, and `search_tools` could not find them.
  The DM page now skips a DM that became a colony, and a resume that lands in a colony's own session
  keeps the phase that session saved. A colony already caught this way runs as a colony again once
  its session's `meta.json` says `"phase": "colony"`.
- **A test run can no longer write into a real Hive home.** Importing litellm runs `load_dotenv()`,
  which applied the repository's `.env` and its `HIVE_HOME` inside the test process.
  `core/conftest.py` and `tools/tests/conftest.py` now point `HIVE_HOME` at a throwaway directory
  before anything imports Hive or litellm.

## For code built on Hive

- New endpoints: `GET`/`PUT /api/queen/{id}/soul`, `POST /api/queen/{id}/soul/reflect`,
  `POST /api/queen/{id}/soul/rollback`, `POST /api/queen/{id}/soul/proposals/{pid}/decide` and
  `.../probe`; `GET`/`PUT /api/queen/{id}/persona` (fields replaced whole),
  `GET /api/queen/{id}/overview`; `POST /api/queen/{id}/remove` and `.../restore`,
  `GET /api/queen/removed`.
- `queen_profiles.list_queens()` leaves out the queens removed from the catalog
  (`queens/catalog.json`); `list_removed_queens()`, `set_queen_removed()` and `removed_queen_ids()`
  read and change it.
- `framework.config.get_soul_config()` and `SoulConfig`; `framework.agents.queen.soul` stores a soul
  and renders it for the prompt; `SessionManager.spawn_background()` runs a task beside the server
  that shutdown gives a moment to finish.

`aden-tools` is released at the same version. It hasn't changed, but the two packages pin each
other exactly.
